Source & scope

Public transactions from a UK-based online retailer, across all included customer countries. Analysis uses twelve complete months, 1 Dec 2010–30 Nov 2011. The source's final partial December is excluded.

UCI Online Retail · Chen, D. (2015) · DOI: 10.24432/C5BW33 · CC BY 4.0.

Each browser data row is a SKU × calendar month × country aggregate. No customer IDs, personal contact details or original invoice lines are embedded in this dashboard.

Prepared by James You with AI-assisted development. Guided SQL practice and source decisions are recorded in the repository. This case study uses no private employer data.

What the metrics mean

  • Gross sales: included positive, priced merchandise entries before credits.
  • Orders: distinct positive-sale invoices, not invoice-line counts.
  • Signed value: gross sales less included credits; not profit or recognised accounting revenue.
  • ABC: full-period sales contribution, with the threshold-crossing SKU kept in the higher-priority class.
  • Original repeated rows remain flagged and retained because there is no unique source invoice-line key.

Observed stockouts, margins, financial inventory turnover and realised savings cannot be measured from these fields.
