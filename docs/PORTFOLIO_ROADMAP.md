## Project 3: E-commerce Profitability & Pricing Analytics

Planned repository: `ecommerce-profitability-analytics`. No real Etsy data has been retrieved or published for this project.

| Milestone | Exit criterion | Indicative effort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Privacy and accounting plan | Agree what can be disclosed; define currency, fees, freight, refunds and cost timing | 3–5 hours |
| Reconciled model | Revenue, platform fees, advertising, freight, product cost and currency conversion reconcile | 7–10 hours |
| Pricing analysis | Contribution margin, large-item shipping exposure and discount scenarios clearly defined | 5–8 hours |
| Report and release | Actual dashboard, business review, approved public extract and independent walkthrough | 8–12 hours |

Before any public extract, remove customer names, addresses, emails, phone numbers, order identifiers, tracking references, supplier identity and sensitive cost details. Replacing a name with a code does not automatically make a rare order anonymous. Review small groups and combinations of dates, country and product. Consider monthly SKU aggregates or a fully synthetic demonstration. Keep exchange-rate assumptions explicit and distinguish reimbursement of prepaid costs from the allocation of profit.

Publishing real business data, changing existing visibility, deleting repositories or replacing important existing files requires James's explicit agreement. The planned privacy review must produce a concrete extract for approval before anything sensitive is published.
